Join us for our next Sugar Market Academy webinar, where we’ll take a practical, end-to-end look at form capture setup, submission behavior, and visitor tracking in Sugar Market. Forms and landing pages play an important role in capturing prospect and customer information, but creating the form is only one part of the process. Ensuring submissions are captured correctly, fields behave as expected, and visitor activity is properly tracked requires the right setup and validation from beginning to end.
