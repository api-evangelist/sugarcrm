# SugarAI

> SugarAI (formerly SugarCRM) is the Precision Selling Platform — a B2B CRM that uses AI and ERP data to help sellers know where to focus, who to engage, and what to do next. 4,000+ customers across 120+ countries.

SugarAI is built for mid-to-large B2B organizations with complex account management, large product catalogs, and repeat-purchase customer bases. It integrates natively with 180+ ERP systems to surface buying behavior changes, revenue risks, and expansion opportunities. Nucleus Research found 32% lower total cost of ownership compared to Salesforce.

## Products

- [Sugar Sell](https://sugarai.com/product/sugar-sell): AI-powered sales automation with guided selling, pipeline management, and account intelligence
- [Sugar Sales Intelligence](https://sugarai.com/product/sugar-sales-intelligence): Revenue intelligence from ERP data — buying behavior analysis, account risk detection, and expansion opportunities
- [Sugar Market](https://sugarai.com/product/sugar-market): Marketing automation with multi-channel campaigns, lead scoring, and analytics
- [Sugar Serve](https://sugarai.com/product/sugar-serve): Customer service platform with case management and self-service portals
